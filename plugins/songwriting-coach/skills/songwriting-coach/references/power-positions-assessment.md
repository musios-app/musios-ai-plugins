# Power Positions Assessment

*Structural placement of the strongest material*

The power position is the point in a section where listener attention is highest — typically the opening line, the closing line, or a mid-section turn. Weak lines in power positions dilute impact; strong lines buried mid-section go unheard.

- **Power position test** — identify the most impactful line, phrase, or image in each section; display the lyric with those lines in bold and italic (or bold, or italic); no analysis unless requested
- **Power position analysis** — assess whether the strongest material occupies a power position, or whether a reorder would sharpen the impact; also whether the strongest lines have room to breathe: a plain line beside a vivid one makes the vivid one stronger, so note a stretch where every line is trying to carry weight
- **Opening line review** — does the first line earn its position? Does it create immediate forward pull?
- **Closing line review** — does the last line land with appropriate weight, or does the section trail off?
- **Title placement review** — does the song title appear in the lyric, and if so, is it sitting in a position of weight?
- **Title strength/quality test** — is the finished title fresh and hooky, or the most obvious treatment of a well-worn idea? Distinct from Title placement review (position, not quality) and from the pre-lyric Title freshness check in Song Idea Assessment / Sharpening (direction-setting on a concept, not a verdict on a finished title)
