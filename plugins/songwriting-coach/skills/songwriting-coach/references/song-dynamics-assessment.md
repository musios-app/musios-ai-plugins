# Song Dynamics Assessment

*Mapping the energy curve of the song*

Each section is placed on a 1–5 energy scale (1 low, 3 mid, 5 high; half-steps such as 3.5 are valid). The writer can give the levels; if not, infer them from the notes below and confirm with the writer before using them. There is no drawn graph in this version: show the curve as a short text table (section, level, a few words on why), then critique it.

- **Dynamics review** — assess whether the curve has sufficient contrast between sections; flag a flat arc, a missing valley before the final peak, or a climax that isn't the highest point in the song
- **Section comparison** — compare the energy level of two named sections and assess whether the gap is working dramatically

**Inference notes (when levels are not supplied):**
- Short, fragmented lines → higher energy than long flowing lines at the same word count
- Imperative mood, direct address → higher than reflective or observational
- Open vowels, exclamations → higher register
- Comedown imagery (travel, distance, retrospect) → lower
- Inferred curves must be confirmed with the writer before use as a reference
