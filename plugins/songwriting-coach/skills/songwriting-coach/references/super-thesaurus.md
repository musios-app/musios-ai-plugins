# Super-Thesaurus

*Explore words, rhymes, and phrasing*

- **Word/image mining** — a field of related words, images, or sounds around a concept for you to draw from
- **Rhyme field** — candidate rhymes (near and perfect) for a target word, with phonetic notes
- **Alternate phrasing** — 2–3 ways a specific idea could be expressed differently, for you to accept, reject, or cannibalise
- **Title candidates** — title options from a theme or key phrase
